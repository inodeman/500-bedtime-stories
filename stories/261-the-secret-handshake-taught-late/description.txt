The Secret Handshake Taught Late is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a schoolyard with a low swing and a circle of stones, Opal is just outside the fun, close to a handshake a new friend had not been taught. Ilaria changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Opal has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Cousin Pio lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friendship makes room before it keeps score.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
