The Name Written on Someone's Sketch | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Name Written on Someone's Sketch is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table with jam, butter, and a shared pencil tin, Cosima is tangled up with a name written by accident on someone else's sketch and almost hides it. Cosima tells Ms. Okonkwo the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Hiding a dent does not make the dent untrue.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
