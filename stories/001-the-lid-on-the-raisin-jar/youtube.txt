The Lid on the Raisin Jar | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Lid on the Raisin Jar is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bakery kitchen where dough and jam are left to finish, Dahlia wants to check on a raisin jar with the loose tin lid before it is ready. With Mama nearby, Dahlia leaves it covered and does a quiet task instead, until it finishes in its own time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Patience lets a small thing finish its quiet work.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
