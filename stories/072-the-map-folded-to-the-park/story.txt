The mild morning air touched the bakery windows with a soft warmth that made the glass feel almost sleepy under the pale light. Sora stepped inside first and felt the change from outdoor coolness to the gentle indoor heat rising from the ovens. They carried the map tucked under one arm and paused near the entrance while the busy morning sounds of soft footsteps and quiet orders filled the space. Lumen came right beside Sora and brushed a sleeve against theirs in greeting. Ms. Okonkwo stood behind the counter arranging trays, and the light from the front window fell across her hands in a calm golden band. Sora noticed how the air held a faint friendly warmth that settled on their cheeks like a light scarf. They walked farther in, hearing the low murmur of other early visitors choosing their breakfasts, and felt glad the day had begun so gently for everyone gathered there.

Sora reached the bakery counter and carefully unfolded the map open to the park so the green paths and little drawings of trees showed clearly on the smooth wood surface. The paper felt slightly crisp under their fingertips as they smoothed one corner flat. Lumen leaned close to look at the open park section too. A tin clock on the high shelf gave a steady soft tick that marked the quiet minutes. Ms. Okonkwo smiled and asked what they might like this morning. Sora glanced at the rows of fresh rolls and fruit tarts waiting under the glass. The map stayed spread between them, its park side facing up like an invitation for later walking. Sora rested one hand lightly on the paper edge and felt how solid the counter was beneath it all.

Ms. Okonkwo lifted a large warm cinnamon roll onto a plate and began to slide it toward Sora first. The roll gave off a sweet steam that curled upward in the mild light. Sora looked at the map folded open to the park and then at Lumen standing quietly nearby. “It will not feel fair if only one person is served first,” Sora said in a calm voice. They reached out and gently touched the edge of the plate. Lumen watched with wide quiet eyes. The bakery counter stayed busy with soft clinks of forks and plates being set down farther along. Sora kept their hand steady on the map so it would not slide while they thought about the share.

Sora asked if the large roll could be cut into two smaller even pieces so both could have some. “Please make a smaller even share for Lumen too,” Sora said clearly. Ms. Okonkwo nodded once and brought a clean knife that caught a little light along its side. She divided the roll carefully down the middle while the warm smell of baking still drifted around them. Sora felt the polished pebble they kept in their pocket press smoothly against their fingers when they reached inside for a moment of quiet thinking. Lumen smiled and accepted the smaller piece when it came. The map remained open between them showing the park paths that waited for later. Other customers moved gently past without hurry.

“Thank you for including me,” Lumen said softly while holding the smaller piece of roll. Sora answered, “We can look at the park together on the map while we eat.” They both took small bites and tasted the gentle sweetness. Ms. Okonkwo set two neat napkins beside the plates without saying more about the choice. She simply let it stand and turned to fold a few more napkins so the stitched pear on each one faced neatly upward. The light from the window stayed mild across the counter and warmed the back of Sora’s hands. A faint smell of basil drifted from a small herb pot near the savory shelf and mixed pleasantly with the bread scent. Sora breathed it in once and felt settled.

Sora traced one path on the open map with a careful finger while finishing their half of the roll. The paper felt dry and smooth under the slow movement. Lumen pointed to a little drawn bench beside a pond drawing. “We could sit there when we go,” Lumen said. Sora nodded and replied, “Yes, and we can share the last bites then too.” Ms. Okonkwo finished folding the pear napkins and placed them in a tidy stack. The busy morning continued around them with quiet voices choosing pastries and the soft scrape of a chair being pulled out nearby. Sora felt the comfortable temperature of the bakery holding steady like a kind blanket over the whole room. They kept the map open so both could see the park clearly.

After the last crumbs were gone Sora folded one corner of the map inward just a little to keep it tidy yet still mostly open to the park. The paper made a tiny soft sound as it creased. Lumen helped by holding the opposite edge steady. Ms. Okonkwo wiped a small spot on the counter until it shone again under the morning light. Sora noticed how the light had grown a shade brighter yet stayed gentle through the glass. They slipped the polished pebble deeper into the pocket where it rested cool and round against the fabric. Everything around the bakery counter felt unhurried even though people came and went with their own bags and boxes. Sora stood close to Lumen and felt the simple gladness of the shared pieces.

Sora and Lumen stepped a little aside so new visitors could reach the counter easily. The map stayed folded open enough for both to glance at the park paths once more. “Shall we walk toward the park after this?” Sora asked. Lumen answered, “Yes, the paths look friendly today.” Ms. Okonkwo gave them a small wave from behind the counter and continued her quiet work without any extra words about what had happened. The air near the doorway carried a cooler thread from outside that brushed Sora’s wrists. They held the map together at the edges and felt how light it was between their hands. The busy morning softened into a slower pace as fewer people lined up. Sora noticed the calm way the light settled on the wooden floorboards in long mild stripes.

Sora slipped the map carefully under one arm again while Lumen held the door. The mild outdoor air met them with its soft temperature against their faces as they stepped onto the sidewalk. They walked a few calm paces together and already pictured the green park paths waiting ahead. Behind them the bakery stayed busy in its gentle way. The lantern by the window glowed softly. Sora felt the day stretch quietly forward with room for both of them and the open park still fresh in mind.
