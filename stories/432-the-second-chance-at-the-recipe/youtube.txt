The Second Chance at the Recipe | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Second Chance at the Recipe is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom with the hall light still on, Greta notices that Grandma Nia has already taken care of a second chance at a recipe without a sigh. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Greta does one useful thing Grandma Nia did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing who helped, and helping back, keeps a home kind.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
