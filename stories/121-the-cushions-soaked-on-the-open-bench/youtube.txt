The Cushions Soaked on the Open Bench | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Cushions Soaked on the Open Bench is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage porch and the hall just inside, a cushions left on an open bench as the mist came in is awkward for one person. Yuri notices Cousin Pio struggling and takes a useful half of the job without asking to be praised. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness is helping carry what feels too heavy.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
