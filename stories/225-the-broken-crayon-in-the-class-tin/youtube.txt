The Broken Crayon in the Class Tin | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Broken Crayon in the Class Tin is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage hallway of hooks, rugs, and boots, Pilar is tangled up with a broken crayon returned to the tin as if it were whole and almost hides it. Pilar tells the baker the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Owning a mistake is how trust grows back.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
