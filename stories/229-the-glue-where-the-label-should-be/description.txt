The Glue Where the Label Should Be is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a workshop bench of wood, glue, and labels, Sable is tangled up with a glue blob where a careful label should be and almost hides it. Sable tells Uncle Idris the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A plain truth is kinder than a tidy secret.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
