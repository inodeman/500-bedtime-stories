"Niko, the low swing is free and the stones look inviting," Cousin Pio said in a soft voice as they arrived. Warm afternoon light filtered through the high branches and touched Niko's cheeks with a soft glow while they entered the familiar schoolyard side by side. Niko walked carefully over the firm packed earth and soon noticed Anil standing just beside the plain wooden bench where a single blue mitten had been set to hold a place. The low swing hung still from its frame nearby and the circle of stones rested neatly in the dust. Niko felt the mild heat of the sun lingering on their shoulders and smiled at how peaceful everything looked in that golden light. Anil remained a little apart from the open play space yet close enough to see it all.

Niko stepped closer to the bench and the swing with Pio following at an easy pace. "Would you like to push the swing first, Anil?" Niko asked in a friendly way while the cool edge of a breeze brushed across their forehead. Cousin Pio stood back a few steps and said nothing more, giving the children room to figure things out. Niko glanced at the blue mitten resting on the bench seat and thought about changing how they used the space so Anil would not stay outside. The ground under Niko's shoes felt solid and slightly dusty, and the quiet of the yard wrapped around them all. Niko wanted Anil to have a true spot right away without any fuss.

With careful hands Niko lifted the blue mitten from the middle of the bench and set it safely on the armrest instead. "Come sit here with me, Anil, and we can watch the swing together before we take turns," Niko said while patting the wooden seat. The surface of the bench felt smooth and still warm from the earlier sun under Niko's open palm. Anil walked over and settled beside Niko so they sat shoulder to shoulder looking out at the low swing and the stone circle. Cousin Pio watched from the side with a calm face and let the two friends arrange the seating themselves. Niko sensed the solid wood supporting them and felt the afternoon ease into something shared.

From the bench they could see every stone in the perfect circle and Niko pointed toward them with a slow gesture. "I like the way the stones make a ring," Anil said quietly while looking across the yard. Niko heard the sound of wind in the fig leaves that grew along the far edge of the schoolyard and the leaves whispered in a soft rhythm. The noise was light and leafy and it made Niko breathe slower as they listened. Niko suggested they leave the bench for a time and walk over to the stones at a gentle speed so no one felt rushed. Pio remained where he was and simply smiled as Niko set the new slower pace for everyone.

Niko, Anil, and Pio moved across the open ground until they reached the circle of stones and sat down close to one another on the earth. The smell of cedar rose from the old fence boards that stood near that corner of the yard and Niko drew in the clean woody fragrance. Anil picked up one round stone and turned it slowly. "These stones are cool to the touch," Anil remarked. Niko nodded and they all began shifting a few stones to form a smaller circle inside the first one, taking turns without hurry. The cedar scent stayed in the air around Niko and made the moment feel fresh and settled.

After the stones were arranged Niko rose and led the way back to the low swing with Anil. They took turns sitting on the swing while the other pushed gently. When Niko held the ropes they noticed the fibers felt sturdy and a bit rough against their fingers. "We can all hold the sides and walk it forward," Niko explained so that the three of them could share one long slow movement together instead of separate turns. Cousin Pio stayed a short distance away and allowed the children to try the new idea on their own. Niko felt the gentle motion and the company of friends as the swing crept forward step by step.

When the swinging was done they returned to the area by the bench where someone had earlier unrolled a straw mat. Niko sat cross-legged on the straw mat and ran their hands over its dry woven texture that scratched lightly against their skin. Anil and Pio sat down too so the three filled the mat in an easy group. The mat made a faint crackling sound under their weight as they got comfortable in the growing shade. Niko picked up a leftover stone and turned it over slowly in their palm as a small quiet action while they rested side by side. No one spoke for a while and the schoolyard held them all in calm.

As the light began to fade they stood and started the walk home along the path that left the schoolyard behind. Niko carried the blue mitten now and kept it ready for cooler air. "Thank you for saving me a spot without saying it was special," Anil said to Niko with a warm look. The evening air felt cooler against Niko's face and arms as they walked. Cousin Pio trailed a few paces back and let the friends talk while he kept watch in his quiet way. Niko remembered the changed seating and the shared pace and felt content that Anil had belonged fully the whole time.

Back in their room after supper Niko sat near the window and recalled the schoolyard afternoon with the low swing and the circle of stones. The lantern by the window glowed softly. Its warm yellow light spilled across the sill and mixed with the last pale blue of the sky outside while Niko pulled a knitted blanket up around their shoulders. The room air felt still and mildly cool now that night had nearly come. Niko thought of Anil sitting close on the bench and on the mat and of Pio standing back so they could solve the small things alone. With eyes growing heavy Niko listened to the hush of the house and drifted toward sleep in that gentle light.
