The How the Chorus Decides the Key is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table before breakfast, chairs close, Mama begins to tell Ilaria about how the chorus decides the key. Ilaria wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Listening all the way through is a gift you can give today.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
