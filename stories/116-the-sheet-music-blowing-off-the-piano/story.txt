The soft evening breeze touched the cottage porch gently, cooling the wooden boards with a mild temperature that felt kind against bare skin after the day. Bibi walked out onto the porch first, feeling the smooth planks under their feet while the last pale light of dusk settled over the rails and the quiet yard. Bibi paused there and drew in a slow breath, smelling the faint wet wool of coats that hung drying near the open door after the earlier light rain. Bibi listened to the leaves stir softly and watched how the breeze played with the grass, then Bibi turned toward the hall just inside where warm light spilled out. Bibi felt glad to be here with family on such a peaceful evening and stepped closer to the doorway with calm steps.

Inside the hall the upright piano stood near the open door so that its polished wood caught the porch air, and Cousin Pio stood beside it trying to gather loose pages. Bibi entered the hall and noticed right away how the soft breeze lifted the sheet music off the piano in awkward fluttering waves that made it hard for one person to catch them all at once. Bibi saw Cousin Pio stretch and reach while several pages drifted toward the porch threshold, and Bibi moved forward without a word of fuss. The air in the hall held a gentle warmth from the nearby kitchen, and Bibi felt the quiet comfort of the cottage settle around them as they watched. Bibi stayed close and ready, hearing the distant hush of the yard outside while the pages danced lightly.

Cousin Pio said, “These pages keep lifting away whenever the breeze comes through,” and reached again with both hands full already. Bibi answered softly, “I can hold this side steady while you gather the others,” and stepped right in to take half the job. Bibi caught two pages that had drifted near the porch edge and felt the thin paper edge under their careful fingers as they pressed the sheets flat against the piano top. Bibi worked quietly beside Cousin Pio so that the awkward lifting became easy shared work, and neither of them hurried. The light from the hall lamp fell in a warm pool across the floorboards and made the white pages easy to see. Bibi kept their movements slow and sure, staying right next to the piano and the open door.

More pages tried to lift, but Bibi and Cousin Pio together brought them down one by one until the stack grew neat again. Bibi said, “We can close the door just a little now so the breeze stays kinder,” and Cousin Pio nodded with a small smile. Cousin Pio replied, “That helps a great deal, Bibi, thank you for noticing,” yet Bibi simply kept placing pages in order without waiting for any extra words. Bibi felt the cool air from the porch mix with the hall’s mild warmth and heard the soft brush of paper against wood. Bibi stayed focused on the useful half of the task, smoothing each sheet so it lay ready for later playing. The cottage felt peaceful around them both as the last loose page settled.

Bibi carried the gathered stack a few steps farther into the hall where the air grew stiller and safer from any breeze. Cousin Pio followed and together they set the sheet music back on the piano rack in a tidy pile. Bibi ran one careful hand along the top page to keep everything flat and listened while a tin clock on the shelf marked the quiet seconds with its steady light tick. Bibi felt how the hall’s wooden floor held a gentle coolness underfoot and noticed the soft glow of evening lamps beginning to strengthen. Bibi and Cousin Pio stood side by side for a moment simply looking at the settled music, and Bibi felt content that the awkward lifting had ended so smoothly. No one needed to say much more because the work was already shared and done.

In the kitchen doorway just beyond the hall the kettle began its quiet tick as it warmed, and Bibi paused to breathe slowly while that small sound joined the calm of the cottage. Cousin Pio said, “Shall we leave the music here ready for tomorrow,” and Bibi answered, “Yes, it looks settled and safe now.” Bibi smelled the clean warmth drifting from the kitchen and felt the soft fabric of their own sleeve against their arm as they rested a hand on the piano. Bibi stayed near Cousin Pio a little longer, enjoying the simple peace that followed the shared task. The light in the hall had grown golden and low, wrapping the piano and the doorway in a gentle glow that made everything feel finished for the evening. Bibi knew the porch waited just outside still cool and quiet.

Bibi walked back toward the porch doorway once more and felt the mild shift in temperature where hall warmth met outdoor air. Cousin Pio joined Bibi there for a last look at the soft dusk settling over the rails and the yard. Bibi listened to the faint hush of leaves and the far-off call of an evening bird while they both stood without hurry. Bibi rested a hand on the door frame and felt its smooth worn wood under their palm. The sheet music now lay quiet inside where no breeze could lift it, and Bibi felt glad they had taken half the gathering without needing any special notice. The cottage porch held its gentle coolness and the hall behind them kept its steady lamps.

Later Bibi and Cousin Pio returned fully inside and closed the door most of the way so only a soft ribbon of night air remained. Bibi sat for a short while on the hall bench near the piano and felt the sturdy cushion beneath them while the room grew stiller. Cousin Pio brought two cups of warm tea that carried a mild steam into the air, and Bibi held theirs carefully. Bibi watched the steam curl upward in the lamp light and listened to the quiet house settle around them both. Everything felt peaceful after the small useful work they had shared on the porch edge and in the hall. Bibi stayed close to the calm center of the cottage and let the evening deepen slowly.

Bibi finished the tea and set the cup down with care while the last light of day faded completely beyond the porch. Cousin Pio hummed a soft tune that matched nothing in particular yet filled the hall with friendly sound. Bibi leaned back and felt the mild temperature of the room hold steady and kind. The sheets of music rested unmoved on the piano, safe now from any lifting breeze. Bibi thought of how simply taking half the awkward job had made the moment easy for everyone. The lantern by the window glowed softly.
