The Button Sewn with the Wrong Thread is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage hallway of hooks, rugs, and boots, Una is tangled up with a button sewn on with loud thread that was not the match and almost hides it. Una tells the baker the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Owning a mistake is how trust grows back.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
