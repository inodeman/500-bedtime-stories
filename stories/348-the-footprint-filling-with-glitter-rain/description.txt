The Footprint Filling with Glitter Rain is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom window on a frosty morning, Gita finds a footprint in garden soil filling with fine rain. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Gita shows Maren how to look without disturbing it. Auntie June looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Not every treasure needs to be pocketed.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
