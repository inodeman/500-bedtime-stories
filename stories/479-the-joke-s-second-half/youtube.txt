The Joke's Second Half | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Joke's Second Half is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a porch while rain ticks and nobody is late, Nana Ruth begins to tell Dori about a joke whose second half was the real joke. Dori wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Quiet attention can be warmer than advice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
