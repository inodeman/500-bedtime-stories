The Torn Page Corner | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Torn Page Corner is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ceramic studio sill above the quiet plaza, Chioma is tangled up with a page corner torn by a careless turn and almost hides it. Chioma tells Grandpa Sol the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth early makes a small mistake easier to mend.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
