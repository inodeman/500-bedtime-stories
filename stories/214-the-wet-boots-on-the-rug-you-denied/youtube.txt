The Wet Boots on the Rug You Denied | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Wet Boots on the Rug You Denied is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a workshop bench of wood, glue, and labels, Pema is tangled up with a wet boots and a rug you almost blamed on the cat and almost hides it. Pema tells Uncle Idris the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A plain truth is kinder than a tidy secret.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
