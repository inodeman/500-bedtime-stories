The Clean Paintbrushes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a garden row with string and two watering cans, a paintbrushes rinsed while someone read the color names will go wrong if one person rushes. Yael and Wynn split it into two slow jobs, while Miss Dalca only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Finishing together matters more than finishing first.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
