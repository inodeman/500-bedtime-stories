The Puppet Show Missing a Voice | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Puppet Show Missing a Voice is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom rug of cushions, puppets, and paper, Zev is just outside the fun, close to a puppet that had no voice yet. Isla changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Zev has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Papa lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Staying beside someone is sometimes the whole gift.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
