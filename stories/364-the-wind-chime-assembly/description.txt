The Wind Chime Assembly is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a harbor dock where baskets are passed up, a wind chime whose strings needed one person to hold and one to knot will go wrong if one person rushes. Farah and Juno split it into two slow jobs, while Uncle Idris only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Two careful pairs of hands can do what one rush cannot.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
