The Mud on the Ferry Cushion | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Mud on the Ferry Cushion is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table with jam, butter, and a shared pencil tin, Idris is tangled up with a mud on the ferry cushion from your heel and almost hides it. Idris tells Ms. Okonkwo the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Hiding a dent does not make the dent untrue.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
