The Choir Folder Mix-Up is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage room with a long tablecloth and paper stars, a choir folders that had been shelved by the wrong color will go wrong if one person rushes. Eko and Casper split it into two slow jobs, while the baker only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Asking for a second pair of hands is a skill.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
