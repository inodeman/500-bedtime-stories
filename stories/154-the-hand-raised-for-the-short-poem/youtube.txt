The Hand Raised for the Short Poem | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Hand Raised for the Short Poem is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a lantern walk along the evening lane, Dori feels shy about a hand that could go up for a four-line poem. Instead of pretending not to care, Dori tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Nana Ruth stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A shy try, done kindly, is already courage.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
