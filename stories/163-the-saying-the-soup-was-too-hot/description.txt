The Saying the Soup Was Too Hot is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library story circle, Bodhi feels shy about a truth that the soup was still too hot for a little cousin. Instead of pretending not to care, Bodhi tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Auntie June stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Being new at something is not the same as failing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
