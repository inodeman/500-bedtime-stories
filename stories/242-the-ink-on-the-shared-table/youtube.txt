The Ink on the Shared Table | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Ink on the Shared Table is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table with jam, butter, and a shared pencil tin, Golda is tangled up with an ink spot on the shared table and almost hides it. Golda tells Ms. Okonkwo the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Hiding a dent does not make the dent untrue.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
