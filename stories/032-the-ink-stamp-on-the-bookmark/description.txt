The Ink Stamp on the Bookmark is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage windowsill of jars, trays, and drying wool, Greta wants to check on a bookmark waiting for the ink stamp to dry before it is ready. With the ferryman nearby, Greta leaves it covered and does a quiet task instead, until it finishes in its own time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting kindly is a way of taking care.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
