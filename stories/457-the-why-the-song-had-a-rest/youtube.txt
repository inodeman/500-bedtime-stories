The Why the Song Had a Rest | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Why the Song Had a Rest is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library window seat, the ferryman begins to tell Remy about explanation of why a song needed a rest in the middle. Remy wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A story belongs to the person still speaking it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
