The Moss Pillow on the North Stone | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Moss Pillow on the North Stone is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a meadow fence and a bucket of water, Pilar finds a moss pillow on the stone that never got noon. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Pilar shows Tala how to look without disturbing it. Auntie Luz looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Showing someone how to look is a kind of gift.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
