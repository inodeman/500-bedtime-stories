The Bread Crust Map is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a garden path after a gentle rain, Chioma finds a bread crust with a pattern like a coastline. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Chioma shows Delia how to look without disturbing it. Mama looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Wonder grows when you look without grabbing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
