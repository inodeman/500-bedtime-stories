The Walk Timed to the Baker's Window is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom with the hall light still on, Golda notices that Grandma Nia has already taken care of a walk timed so you would see the baker's window lit. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Golda does one useful thing Grandma Nia did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing who helped, and helping back, keeps a home kind.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
