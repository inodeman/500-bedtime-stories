The Asking Papa to Listen | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Asking Papa to Listen is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library story circle, Zola feels shy about asking Papa to listen before the day ended. Instead of pretending not to care, Zola tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Auntie June stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Being new at something is not the same as failing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
