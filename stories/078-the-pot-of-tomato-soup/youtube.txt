The Pot of Tomato Soup | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Pot of Tomato Soup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ferry bench with a folded blanket, Yelena sees that a pot of tomato soup with a second bowl nearby will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Yelena makes a smaller, even share so Zia is included, and Miss Dalca lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Making room at the table is a kind of abundance.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
