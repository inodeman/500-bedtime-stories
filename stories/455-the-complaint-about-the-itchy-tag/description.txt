The Complaint about the Itchy Tag is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom at lamp time, story not started yet, Auntie Luz begins to tell Isla about explanation of an itchy tag that took a while to get to the point. Isla wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: The best answer sometimes starts with hearing the whole thing.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
