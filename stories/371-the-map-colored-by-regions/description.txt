The Map Colored by Regions is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a school hall being set for a gentle fair, a big map colored so the edges matched will go wrong if one person rushes. Niko and Anil split it into two slow jobs, while Grandpa Sol only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A fiddly job goes well when two people take slow halves.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
