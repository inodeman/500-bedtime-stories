The Flower Picked without Asking is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table with jam, butter, and a shared pencil tin, Femi is tangled up with a flower picked from a pot that was not yours and almost hides it. Femi tells Ms. Okonkwo the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Hiding a dent does not make the dent untrue.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
