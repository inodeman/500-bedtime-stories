The Kettle Filled before the Ask is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a harbor step where parcels change hands, a kettle that was empty just before tea is awkward for one person. Tala notices Papa struggling and takes a useful half of the job without asking to be praised. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Helping without being asked is a quiet sort of brave.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
