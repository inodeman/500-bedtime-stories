The Blanket on the Ferry Bench | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Blanket on the Ferry Bench is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a school cloakroom and the lunch mats just beyond, Delia sees that a folded blanket on the ferry bench will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Delia makes a smaller, even share so Ilaria is included, and Uncle Idris lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: What is split with care does not become less important.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
