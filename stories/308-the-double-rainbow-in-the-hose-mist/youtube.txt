The Double Rainbow in the Hose Mist | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Double Rainbow in the Hose Mist is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom window on a frosty morning, Kito finds a small rainbow in the hose mist. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Kito shows Hadi how to look without disturbing it. Auntie June looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Not every treasure needs to be pocketed.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
