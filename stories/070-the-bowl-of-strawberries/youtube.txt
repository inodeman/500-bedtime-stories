The Bowl of Strawberries | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Bowl of Strawberries is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a picnic cloth in a calm meadow, Ines sees that a bowl of strawberries still cool from the rinse will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Ines makes a smaller, even share so Nia is included, and the baker lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Saving a turn for someone else keeps the joy whole.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
