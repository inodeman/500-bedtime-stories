The Mug the Way You Like It | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Mug the Way You Like It is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library corner with a paper ribbon in a book, Kavi notices that Mr. Ade has already taken care of a mug prepared the way you like it, not the way it is faster. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Kavi does one useful thing Mr. Ade did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A thank-you that takes effort feels true.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
