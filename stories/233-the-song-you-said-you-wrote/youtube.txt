The Song You Said You Wrote | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Song You Said You Wrote is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library table by the round window, Kito is tangled up with a tune you said you wrote, though you had heard it next door and almost hides it. Kito tells Miss Dalca the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honest words let a friend help with the repair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
