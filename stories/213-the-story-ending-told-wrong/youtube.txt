The Story Ending Told Wrong | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Story Ending Told Wrong is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library table by the round window, Lior is tangled up with a story ending you had changed to sound braver and almost hides it. Lior tells Miss Dalca the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honest words let a friend help with the repair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
