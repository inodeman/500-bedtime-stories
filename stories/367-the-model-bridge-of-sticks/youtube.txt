The Model Bridge of Sticks | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Model Bridge of Sticks is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bakery kitchen before the early table, a model bridge that stood only when both sides were held and then not will go wrong if one person rushes. Uma and Yael split it into two slow jobs, while Ms. Okonkwo only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is letting each person keep the part they do best.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
