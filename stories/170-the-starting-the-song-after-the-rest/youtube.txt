The Starting the Song After the Rest | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Starting the Song After the Rest is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a music corner with a drum and a recorder, Ines feels shy about starting a song after a rest, when the room was listening. Instead of pretending not to care, Ines tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Auntie Luz stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Speaking up once, softly, can open a door.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
