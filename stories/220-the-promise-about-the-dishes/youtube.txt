The Promise about the Dishes | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Promise about the Dishes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage hallway of hooks, rugs, and boots, Tala is tangled up with a promise about the dishes that had not been kept yet and almost hides it. Tala tells the baker the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Owning a mistake is how trust grows back.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
