The Borrowed Pencil Worn Down | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Borrowed Pencil Worn Down is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a kitchen table with jam, butter, and a shared pencil tin, Greta is tangled up with a borrowed pencil worn shorter than when it left and almost hides it. Greta tells Ms. Okonkwo the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Hiding a dent does not make the dent untrue.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
