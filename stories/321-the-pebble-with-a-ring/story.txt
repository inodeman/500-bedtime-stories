"Look how the stones shine now, Mama," Yuri said, holding Mama's warm hand as they stepped onto the garden path after a gentle rain. The cool damp air touched Yuri's cheeks and made their skin feel fresh and clean. Yuri walked slowly so their shoes made soft sounds on the wet gravel, and Paz stayed close beside them with quiet steps. Faint gold light still hung in the sky and turned every puddle into a small glowing pool. Yuri watched a single drop fall from a leaf and land with a tiny splash. The path curved ahead between the sleeping flowers, and Yuri felt glad to be right there with Mama and Paz in the quiet.

Yuri paused near a stretch of smooth earth where the smell of rain on dust rose gently into the air and filled their nose with a soft earthy sweetness. They crouched down carefully and spotted a small pebble resting alone on the damp ground. The pebble held a perfect white ring that went all the way around its middle like a quiet belt of light. Yuri's fingers hovered close because the wish to pocket it or poke it felt real and strong. Yet Yuri kept their hands still and simply looked. Paz leaned in too, and the cool temperature of the ground seemed to rise a little against their knees. Yuri whispered the discovery without moving the stone at all.

"We can look as long as we want, but we leave it right where it is," Yuri told Paz in a soft voice that barely stirred the air. Paz nodded and settled onto the path beside Yuri so their shoulders almost touched. The pebble's white ring caught a thin band of remaining daylight and glowed against the darker gray of the stone. Yuri felt the smooth coolness of the air around their wrists while they both stayed very still. Mama stood a step behind them and watched without speaking yet. A light breeze moved through the nearby bushes and carried the quiet rustle of wet leaves. Yuri kept their gaze steady on the ring and felt wonder settle over them like a gentle blanket.

Yuri showed Paz how to tilt their head just so and notice the tiny sparkles trapped inside the white circle without ever reaching out. "See how the ring goes all the way around without a break," Yuri said, tracing the shape in the air above the pebble with one careful finger. Paz answered, "It looks like a little moon that decided to rest here." The two friends stayed kneeling while the temperature of the evening cooled another degree and made the damp path feel softer under them. Mama smiled at the calm way Yuri guided Paz. Nothing hurried them. The light grew softer and pinker across the garden beds as they continued to look.

Mama knelt down too so her coat brushed the wet ground and the smooth texture of a satin ribbon on her sleeve brushed lightly against Yuri's arm. Yuri felt that cool satin ribbon for a moment and smiled at the gentle surprise of it. "I like how you both look with such care," Mama said in a low warm tone that matched the hush of the path. Yuri leaned a little closer to Mama while still keeping eyes on the pebble. The white ring seemed even clearer now that three pairs of eyes rested on it together. A single bird called once from a distant tree and then fell silent again. Yuri breathed in the quiet and felt the shared looking become the best part of the whole evening.

They stayed that way for several slow breaths while the last pale light slid across the pebble and made the white ring shine like a soft band of milk. Yuri felt the cool air on the back of their neck and knew the wish to take the stone still floated nearby, yet the stronger wish was to leave everything exactly as found. Paz reached out once as if to touch and then drew the hand back again, copying Yuri's earlier example. Mama rested a gentle palm on Yuri's shoulder. The garden path held them all in its damp peaceful circle, and no one needed to speak for a little while. Yuri simply watched and felt full of quiet happiness.

At last Yuri stood and brushed the damp bits of gravel from their knees while the soft click of a gate latch sounded from the far end of the garden as someone closed it for the night. "We can come back tomorrow and see if the ring still shines the same," Yuri said to Paz and Mama. They all turned together and began the slow walk toward the house, leaving the pebble resting untouched on the path where the rain had placed it. The air felt cooler now against Yuri's hands, and the last drops fell from the higher branches with tiny taps. Yuri looked back once more at the spot and felt glad the white ring would stay exactly where it belonged. Paz skipped one quiet step and then matched Yuri's pace again.

Inside the house Yuri pressed a dry finger to the cool window glass and traced a slow circle of fog that bloomed under the touch and then faded. Mama helped them out of the damp shoes while Paz waited nearby with a shared smile. The kitchen smelled faintly of warm bread, and the light from the hall lamps felt golden and kind on Yuri's face. They talked in low voices about the pebble and its perfect ring while hanging the coats. Yuri's fingers still remembered the cool air of the path and the way the white circle had held everyone's attention without needing to be moved. Everything felt peaceful and finished in the best way.

Later Yuri climbed into bed under the soft covers while Mama sat close and Paz waved goodnight from the doorway. The room held a gentle hush broken only by the quiet settling of the house. Yuri's eyes grew heavy as they remembered the cool path and the pebble left safe in its place. The lantern by the window glowed softly. Yuri breathed out one long calm breath and let the day fold itself away into sleep.
