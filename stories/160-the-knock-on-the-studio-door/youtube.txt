The Knock on the Studio Door | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Knock on the Studio Door is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a music corner with a drum and a recorder, Eko feels shy about a knock on a studio door that was already ajar. Instead of pretending not to care, Eko tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Auntie Luz stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Speaking up once, softly, can open a door.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
