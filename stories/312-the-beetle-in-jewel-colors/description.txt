The Beetle in Jewel Colors is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a harbor wall at low tide, Femi finds a beetle green as a bottle, crossing a leaf. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Femi shows Ren how to look without disturbing it. the ferryman looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small beautiful thing can be shared with the eyes only.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
