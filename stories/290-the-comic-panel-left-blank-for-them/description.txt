The Comic Panel Left Blank for Them is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom rug of cushions, puppets, and paper, Jasper is just outside the fun, close to a comic panel left blank on purpose. Kei changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Jasper has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Papa lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Staying beside someone is sometimes the whole gift.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
