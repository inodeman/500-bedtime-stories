The Shortened Nap You Called Finished | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Shortened Nap You Called Finished is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a workshop bench of wood, glue, and labels, Kira is tangled up with a nap you shortened and then called finished and almost hides it. Kira tells Uncle Idris the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A plain truth is kinder than a tidy secret.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
