The How the Crack Got in the Step is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage hearth with the clock unhurried, Auntie June begins to tell Bodhi about how the crack got in the step, which was a longer story than the crack. Bodhi wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting your turn to talk lets someone feel heard.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
