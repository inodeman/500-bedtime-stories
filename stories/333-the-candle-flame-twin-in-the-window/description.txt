The Candle Flame Twin in the Window is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom window on a frosty morning, Kito finds a candle and its twin in the dark window. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Kito shows Golda how to look without disturbing it. Auntie June looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Not every treasure needs to be pocketed.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
