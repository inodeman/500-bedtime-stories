The Fingerprint Whorl | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Fingerprint Whorl is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bedroom window on a frosty morning, Kavi finds a fingerprint whorl in flour on the table. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Kavi shows Cleo how to look without disturbing it. Auntie June looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Not every treasure needs to be pocketed.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
