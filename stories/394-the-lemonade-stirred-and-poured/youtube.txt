The Lemonade Stirred and Poured | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Lemonade Stirred and Poured is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a harbor dock where baskets are passed up, a lemonade stirred by one and poured by the other will go wrong if one person rushes. Esme and Teo split it into two slow jobs, while Uncle Idris only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Two careful pairs of hands can do what one rush cannot.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
