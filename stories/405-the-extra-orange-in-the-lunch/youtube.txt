The Extra Orange in the Lunch | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Extra Orange in the Lunch is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a workshop with tools on a low peg, Ximena notices that Papa has already taken care of an extra orange tucked in beside the sandwich. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Ximena does one useful thing Papa did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Gratitude shows up in what you do next.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
