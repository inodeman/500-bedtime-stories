The Clay Thumbprint on the Good Cup | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Clay Thumbprint on the Good Cup is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ceramic studio sill above the quiet plaza, Bibi is tangled up with a thumbprint pressed into a cup that was meant to be smooth and almost hides it. Bibi tells Grandpa Sol the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Telling the truth early makes a small mistake easier to mend.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
