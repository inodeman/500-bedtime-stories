The Tools Put Back Where You Could Reach is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a harbor shelter on the lee side of the wind, Paz notices that Neighbor Sami has already taken care of a tools put back on the low peg. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Paz does one useful thing Neighbor Sami did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Remembering a kindness is a way of returning it.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
