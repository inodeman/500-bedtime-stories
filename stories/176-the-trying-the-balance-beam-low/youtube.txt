The Trying the Balance Beam Low | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Trying the Balance Beam Low is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a small school room with a low stage edge, Nils feels shy about a low balance line taped on the floor. Instead of pretending not to care, Nils tries the smallest brave version in a voice that can be heard, while Mama stays kind and does not push for a show. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Courage can be a small voice used at the right moment.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
