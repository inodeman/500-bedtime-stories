The Mitten String Shared Between Hooks is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a school cloakroom and the lunch mats just beyond, Esme sees that a mitten string long enough for two hooks will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Esme makes a smaller, even share so Teo is included, and Uncle Idris lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: What is split with care does not become less important.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
