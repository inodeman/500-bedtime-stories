"Would you like to sit here with me on the folded blanket?" Vera said to Uma when they found the ferry bench near the railing. Soft amber light poured over the wooden deck and warmed Vera's cheeks as they climbed up and made a careful space. A second pair of puddle boots stood neatly by the small cabin door, their yellow sides catching the glow like friendly lanterns of their own. Vera unfolded the blanket just enough so both children could tuck their legs under the thick cloth. The ferry glided forward with a low steady murmur that Vera found soothing. They patted the spot beside them until Uma settled in close. Miss Dalca stood a short distance away with a calm face and a covered basket. Vera felt the smooth grain of the bench under one hand and kept their body turned toward Uma so the friend felt welcome right away.

Vera gazed at the second pair of puddle boots by the door and thought about how turns sometimes went. Cool evening air brushed across Vera's bare arms with a pleasant freshness that made them sit up a little taller. Uma pointed at the boots with quiet interest, and Vera understood at once that it would not feel fair if only one person was served first with a full chance to wear them. Vera began to plan a smaller even share that would include Uma from the very beginning. The light shifted to a deeper gold across the water. Miss Dalca opened her basket slowly. Vera listened to the gentle creak of ropes and stayed pressed near Uma on the folded blanket. They decided the boots and whatever came from the basket would both be shared with care.

Miss Dalca's hands smelled of lemon soap when she unwrapped the warm biscuits and set them down near Vera. The second pair of puddle boots remained by the door in the soft shadow of the cabin wall while Vera watched. The biscuits rested on a floured towel whose powdery surface felt dry and fine when Vera brushed it with one fingertip. Pale light danced on the biscuit tops. Vera broke the largest biscuit into four smaller even pieces so that Uma could have the same amount at the same time. Miss Dalca saw the careful dividing and simply waited. Vera offered two little pieces to Uma first and kept two for themself. The ferry rocked them both in a slow rhythm that matched their calm breathing.

"These pieces are the same size for both of us," Vera said to Uma while the wind in the fig leaves on the nearby shore whispered a dry and gentle song. Uma took the share and smiled with biscuit crumbs at the corners of their mouth. Vera tasted the plain warm flavor and felt glad. They looked again toward the second pair of puddle boots by the door and knew the same even spirit should guide the boots too. The air held a mild chill that made the blanket feel even better. Miss Dalca poured a little water into two small cups. Vera waited until Uma had sipped before they drank. The folded blanket stayed tucked around their legs like a shared nest.

"May Uma and I each try one boot for a short turn at the same time?" Vera asked Miss Dalca in a clear quiet voice. The bench boards felt solid and slightly rough under Vera's shoes when they stood. Miss Dalca gave a single kind nod and moved aside without any extra words. Vera and Uma walked together to the cabin door where the second pair of puddle boots waited. Soft light from the deck lamps touched the yellow rubber. Vera picked up the left boot and Uma picked up the right so neither went first alone. The boots felt cool and smooth against their palms. They each slipped one foot inside just far enough to know the soft lining, then stepped out again in the same even way and returned to the bench.

Back under the folded blanket Vera made sure the last biscuits became still smaller even crumbs so Uma received exactly the same amount with no leftover unfair piece. The sky above the ferry turned a dusty rose that glowed on Vera's hands and on Uma's hair. Uma giggled softly at the tiny pieces and Vera giggled too in the same gentle way. Miss Dalca let the whole choice stand just as Vera had shaped it, without a speech of any kind at all. Vera felt the gentle motion of the boat ease every tight place in their shoulders and back. The second pair of puddle boots by the door looked ready and peaceful in their matching pair under the lamp. Vera rested against Uma for a moment of shared quiet that needed no words. The water below whispered steady secrets that invited rest and calm.

"I like sharing the boots and the biscuits the smaller way with you," Vera told Uma while drawing slow circles on the wooden edge of the bench with one finger. Cool air slipped under the blanket edges and Vera pulled the cloth higher over both of them. Uma nodded and leaned a warm shoulder against Vera. Miss Dalca packed the cups away with soft clicks. Vera watched the second pair of puddle boots stay side by side by the door as if they too preferred company. The light grew softer and bluer across the open deck. Vera kept Uma included in every glance and every small motion of the quiet ride. The ferry continued its peaceful journey with no hurry at all toward the far bank.

Vera and Uma stayed nestled on the ferry bench while the evening deepened around them with a silvery hush that settled over the deck like a soft shawl. Miss Dalca sat nearby and closed her eyes for a short rest after the quiet sharing. Vera felt the thick folded blanket hold their warmth like a careful hug that wrapped both children together. Uma's breathing slowed to match Vera's in an easy pattern. The second pair of puddle boots by the door waited patiently for the landing that would come soon enough. Vera heard the distant low call of a night bird across the water. They kept one arm around Uma so the friend never felt alone on the moving boat. The boat lights made gentle golden paths on the darkening water that Vera watched with sleepy eyes.

As the ferry neared the quiet dock Vera felt a sleepy comfort fill their whole body from head to toes. The lantern by the window glowed softly. Vera held Uma's hand and looked once more at the second pair of puddle boots by the door that would go with them onto the shore in the same even spirit. Miss Dalca rose and gathered the blanket with slow hands. Vera breathed the cool clean air and listened to the water slap the pilings. They stayed close to Uma while the boat bumped gently into place. Miss Dalca smiled at both children the same way. Vera and Uma stood together ready for whatever small next step waited on land.
