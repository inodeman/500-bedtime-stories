The Tide Pool Eye | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Tide Pool Eye is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a meadow fence and a bucket of water, Ximena finds a tide pool no bigger than a plate, with one shrimp. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Ximena shows Orla how to look without disturbing it. Auntie Luz looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Showing someone how to look is a kind of gift.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
