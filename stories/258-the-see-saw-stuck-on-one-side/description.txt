The See-Saw Stuck on One Side is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a meadow gate where games start, Arun is just outside the fun, close to a see-saw stuck down because the other seat was empty. Vera changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Arun has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Mr. Ade lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Saving a place is a way of saying you belong.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
