The Seed inside the Apple Star is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a meadow fence and a bucket of water, Tala finds a star of seeds inside an apple cut across. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Tala shows Pilar how to look without disturbing it. Auntie Luz looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Showing someone how to look is a kind of gift.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
