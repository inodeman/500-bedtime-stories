The Ink on the Thank-You Note | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Ink on the Thank-You Note is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ceramic studio table where slip and handles must set, Sable wants to check on a thank-you note whose ink was still wet before it is ready. With Nana Ruth nearby, Sable leaves it covered and does a quiet task instead, until it finishes in its own time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A slow finish can be the right finish.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
