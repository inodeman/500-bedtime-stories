The Quilt Corner | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Quilt Corner is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bakery counter on a soft, busy morning, Nim sees that a quilt corner wide enough to lift will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Nim makes a smaller, even share so Yelena is included, and Ms. Okonkwo lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A smaller piece, fairly given, can still be enough.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
