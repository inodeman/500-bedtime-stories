The Chamomile Bundles on the Rack | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Chamomile Bundles on the Rack is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a quiet craft corner with paper, paste, and a wooden rack, Una wants to check on a chamomile bundles on the drying rack before it is ready. With Auntie Luz nearby, Una leaves it covered and does a quiet task instead, until it finishes in its own time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Leaving a cover on is a kind of trust.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
