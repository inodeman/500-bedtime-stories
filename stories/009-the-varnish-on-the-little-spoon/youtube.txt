The Varnish on the Little Spoon | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Varnish on the Little Spoon is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ceramic studio table where slip and handles must set, Paz wants to check on a little wooden spoon wearing wet varnish before it is ready. With Nana Ruth nearby, Paz leaves it covered and does a quiet task instead, until it finishes in its own time. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A slow finish can be the right finish.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
