The Crayons Gathered after the Class is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a school hallway of pegs, stools, and music stands, a crayons left like bright sticks on the floor is awkward for one person. Opal notices Neighbor Sami struggling and takes a useful half of the job without asking to be praised. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Kindness leaves a person with their dignity intact.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
