The Drawing Kept on the Fridge Square | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Drawing Kept on the Fridge Square is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library corner with a paper ribbon in a book, Lior notices that Mr. Ade has already taken care of a drawing kept, not replaced, on the square of the fridge. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Lior does one useful thing Mr. Ade did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A thank-you that takes effort feels true.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
