The Mug of Mint Water is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a courtyard supper with a long table and a fig tree, Ilaria sees that a mug of mint water on the step will not feel fair if only one person is served first. Ilaria makes a smaller, even share so Opal is included, and Grandpa Sol lets the choice stand without a speech. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Sharing a little can make two people feel expected.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
