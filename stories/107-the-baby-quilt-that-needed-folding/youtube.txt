The Baby Quilt That Needed Folding | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Baby Quilt That Needed Folding is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a market lane of baskets, jars, and flower pots, a baby quilt sliding off the line is awkward for one person. Greta notices Grandma Nia struggling and takes a useful half of the job without asking to be praised. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A small useful favor can be louder than a big speech.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
