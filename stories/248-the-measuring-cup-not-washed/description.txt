The Measuring Cup Not Washed is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library table by the round window, Gita is tangled up with a measuring cup put away still sweet and almost hides it. Gita tells Miss Dalca the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honest words let a friend help with the repair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
