The Milk Bottle for the Night Step is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library return desk and the rainy steps, a milk bottle that belonged on the night step is awkward for one person. Lior notices Mr. Ade struggling and takes a useful half of the job without asking to be praised. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Noticing who is tired is already a gentle skill.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
