The Long Tablecloth | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Long Tablecloth is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a school hall being set for a gentle fair, a tablecloth that ballooned if you shook it alone will go wrong if one person rushes. Quinn and Jaleh split it into two slow jobs, while Grandpa Sol only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A fiddly job goes well when two people take slow halves.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
