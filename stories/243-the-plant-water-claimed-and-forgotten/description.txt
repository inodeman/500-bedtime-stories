The Plant Water Claimed and Forgotten is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library table by the round window, Kavi is tangled up with a plant you claimed to have watered and almost hides it. Kavi tells Miss Dalca the plain truth, and together they mend what can be mended, with no teasing. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Honest words let a friend help with the repair.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
