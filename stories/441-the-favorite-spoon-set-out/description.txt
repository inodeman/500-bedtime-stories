The Favorite Spoon Set Out is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage kitchen on an ordinary kind morning, Bibi notices that Cousin Pio has already taken care of a favorite spoon set out, the one with the bird. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Bibi does one useful thing Cousin Pio did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Gratitude is a useful favor, not only a thank-you word.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
