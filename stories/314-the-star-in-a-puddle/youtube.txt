The Star in a Puddle | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Star in a Puddle is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library aisle with a slice of sun, Pema finds a star shaken into pieces by a puddle and then whole again. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Pema shows Ulla how to look without disturbing it. Nana Ruth looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Quiet noticing is a way of being gentle with the world.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
