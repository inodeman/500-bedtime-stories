The Lantern Wick Trimmed | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Lantern Wick Trimmed is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a workshop with tools on a low peg, Tala notices that Papa has already taken care of a lantern wick trimmed so your walk would be even. A quick thank-you feels too thin, so Tala does one useful thing Papa did not ask for and then says, plainly, that they noticed. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Gratitude shows up in what you do next.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
