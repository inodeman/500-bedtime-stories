The Reason the Lanterns Go in That Order | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Reason the Lanterns Go in That Order is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a porch while rain ticks and nobody is late, Nana Ruth begins to tell Delia about reason the lanterns are hung in a certain order. Delia wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Quiet attention can be warmer than advice.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
