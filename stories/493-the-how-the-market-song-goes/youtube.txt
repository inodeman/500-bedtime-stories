The How the Market Song Goes | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The How the Market Song Goes is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a cottage hearth with the clock unhurried, Auntie June begins to tell Zola about a market song that has a silly middle you must not skip. Zola wants to interrupt with bigger news, but listens all the way to the last sentence, asks one real question, and only then shares something small of their own. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Waiting your turn to talk lets someone feel heard.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
