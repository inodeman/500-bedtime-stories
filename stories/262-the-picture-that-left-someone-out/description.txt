The Picture That Left Someone Out is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library rug under the round window, Brigid is just outside the fun, close to a group picture posed before everyone was in it. Nim changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Brigid has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Grandma Nia lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: A game is kinder when nobody has to watch from the step.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
