The Goodbye That Waited for the Second Wave | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Goodbye That Waited for the Second Wave is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a ferry deck with a rail and two elbows of room, Ilaria is just outside the fun, close to a goodbye that waited so two waves could happen. Delia changes the seating, the rules, or the pace so Ilaria has a real place, not a prize announced out loud. Neighbor Sami lets the children solve it. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Friends can change the plan so both can play.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
