The Ice Pattern in the Bird Bath is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a library aisle with a slice of sun, Opal finds an ice pattern like lace on the bird bath, thin and not for stepping. The wish to pocket it or poke it is real, but Opal shows Quinn how to look without disturbing it. Nana Ruth looks too, and they leave it where it is. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Quiet noticing is a way of being gentle with the world.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
