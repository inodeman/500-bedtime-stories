The Stage Blocks | Bedtime Story for Kids in English

The Stage Blocks is a calm bedtime story for children, told in clear English.

At a bakery kitchen before the early table, a three stage blocks that made a step only if aligned will go wrong if one person rushes. Remy and Kito split it into two slow jobs, while Ms. Okonkwo only offers a count or a name for the knot they need. They finish together. The telling is gentle and unhurried, with picture-book scenes and no scares.

Moral: Teamwork is letting each person keep the part they do best.

A read-aloud of about five minutes or more, for ages 4 to 8. From Five Hundred Nights, original English bedtime stories by InodeAi.
